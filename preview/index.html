Make your own train or skyline with AI

Your train

  1. Copy the instructions and paste them into your AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini...) with a photo or a description of the train.
  2. Paste the JSON it gives you here. If there are problems, copy them and hand them back to the AI to fix.
  3. Save it in your profile repository as .github/train.json and use train: .github/train.json.

Your skyline

  1. Copy the instructions and paste them into your AI with the city you want or a photo of its skyline.
  2. Paste the JSON here. These are pixel-art silhouettes: the colors follow the theme on their own.
  3. Save it as .github/skyline.json and use skyline: .github/skyline.json.
