Add your city

Your city's metro, the tram you take to work, the skyline outside your window: if it's real, it belongs here. You don't need to code.

  1. Draw it with AI. Copy the instructions in the playground into your AI with a photo, and paste back the JSON it writes: the playground shows it right away and lists any problems.

  2. Sign it. Add "author" (your GitHub username) and a "description" of the real train or city, and save it as community/trains/<id>.json (the file name is its id) or community/skylines/<city>.json.

  3. Open a pull request. A check validates it the way the Action does and draws a preview in light and dark. Once it's merged, it pulls in here. The depot rules are short: no logos or lettering, a description of the real thing, one train or skyline per pull request.
